The Learning Cycle

How Personalisation Actually Works

Personalisation is not a buzzword in our tagline. It is a systematic process we apply to every lesson, every learner, and every progress review.

01. Understand

Listen First

We start with a conversation about your learner's syllabus, strengths, historical stumbling blocks, and personal goals. No assumptions.

02. Diagnose

Locate the Gaps

Using gentle, targeted diagnostic questions, we uncover why a concept feels difficult instead of simply assigning more repetitive worksheets.

03. Teach & Practise

Model & Reinforce

We teach in step-by-step increments, guide the first attempts, and facilitate deliberate independent practice until competence is solid.

04. Adapt & Report

Specific Progress

Parents receive clear, honest updates on what has improved, what needs practice, and what the next milestone looks like.
